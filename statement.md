# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Campus resources such as projectors, laptops and lab equipment are often handled manually. This makes it difficult to know whether a resource is free, who has borrowed it, and when it is due.

## Scope
The project is a small local command-line system. It manages student records, resource records and bookings. It also checks overdue bookings and creates a CSV report. It is intentionally limited to the basic academic workflow and does not try to become a full college-wide system.

## Target Users
- Students who need to book a shared campus resource
- Lab or department staff who need a simple record of resource usage

## High-Level Features
- Student management
- Resource management
- Booking and return
- Overdue status
- Local file storage
- CSV reporting
